Transcript

$8.58 trillion in federal assistance. Where did it go? And what did the audits find? It's all public, but split across three federal databases, in auditors' language.

This is Audits Made Simple. Federal spending and audit data, made understandable.

Start with the money: interactive Tableau dashboards show where it goes. Ten states received just over half of it.

Stuck on a term like material weakness? The glossary explains it in plain words.

The Audit Health Score turns audit results into one number, from 0 to 100. Red means look closer: a signal, not proof of wrongdoing.

Joined with spending records, the scores show $1.09 trillion went to recipients in the red.

Five students at George Mason built it, and won first place nationally at the AGA Datathon.

Audits Made Simple. Explore it yourself.

Every screen is the live site, recorded frame by frame and played at its real speed; the capture only read and navigated. The browser window around it is redrawn, and the pointer is drawn on its recorded path. A red tier is a screening signal, not proof of wrongdoing.

Audits Made Simple is the work of the Fiscal Patriots, George Mason University: Khaled Alkurd (team lead), Pranavi Doodala (project manager), Mariam Debas (visualization lead), Nikita Chandrasing (product manager) and Andy Yaro (product developer). The Tableau dashboards on screen are the team's.
